## 2. The math of 200K/month

200K clicks/mo ≈ **6,700 clicks/day**. At a healthy portfolio CTR of ~3%, that ≈ 220K impressions/day. What that requires in this niche:

- **~800–1,200 quality pages** live and ranking (articles + fix-guides + comparisons + cheatsheets + glossary + tool pages) — the current 243 is a **starter kit**, not the destination.
- **~2,000–3,000 ranking keywords**, long-tail heavy, plus **30–60 head winners** (error-fix queries + tool queries — our proven strength).
- **2–4 linkable magnets** (tools/data assets) that earn references without paid links.
- Realistic horizon at SAFE velocity: **9–15 months**. (Fast-and-reckless gets you demoted — we already paid that tuition once.)

## 4. Risk controls — the "NEVER AGAIN" rules
1. **Velocity cap 4 pages/week, ever.** Recovery phase = 0.
2. No template/programmatic page families without demonstrable unique value per page.
3. Every external fact verified before ship; internal links = DB-verified exact slugs.

5. Monthly: Manual Actions check + GSC health read + quality audit of 10 random pages.
