    Recommendations

      Strength estimates are heuristic and should not be treated as a security guarantee. Real-world resistance depends on how the password was chosen, where it is reused, and how the site stores it.

      Methodology

      What the checker actually checks

      Transparency over theater — here is the full model, including its limits.

      Positive signals

      Length (the biggest factor), character-type variety, symbol density, and bonuses for 12/16/20+ characters and uppercase letters beyond the first position.

      Pattern penalties

      Common passwords (exact and embedded), dictionary words including leetspeak forms, repeated runs (aaaa), sequences (abcd), keyboard walks (qwerty) and year-like dates.

      Entropy & crack time

      Pool-based entropy (L·log₂N) adjusted downward by detected patterns, then translated into an indicative offline crack time at ~10¹¹ guesses/second.

      Honest limits. A heuristic cannot know how your password was generated or where else you used it. A randomly generated 20-character password will outlive any "clever" human one regardless of what any meter says — including ours. Read how entropy works.
