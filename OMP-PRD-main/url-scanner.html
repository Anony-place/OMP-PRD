Phishing URL & Suspicious Link Risk Scanner

Inspect any suspicious link locally for IDN homograph punycode (xn--), @ authority confusion, brand typosquatting, deceptive .zip TLDs, and open redirects — without visiting the link or sending it to a server.

Frequently asked questions

Is this link safe to open?

No checker can answer that with certainty, but the parts of a link that make it dangerous are easy to flag: lookalike domains, hidden @-authority tricks and open-redirect parameters are the working parts of most phishing campaigns, and this analyzer finds all three locally in your browser. Anything asking for a password, a card or a payment: type the site's address yourself instead of clicking.

What does a phishing link checker actually detect?

The patterns phishing URLs are built from: IDN homograph domains (punycode xn-- lookalikes), typosquatting of well-known brands, @-authority tricks that hide the real destination, deceptive subdomains, open-redirect parameters and other lexical tells. It reads the URL string itself — nothing you paste is sent to a server.

Can a phishing link checker guarantee a site is safe?

No. Lexical analysis catches impersonation; it cannot see a legitimate site that has been compromised or a clean-looking domain used in a live campaign. Treat the result as one signal alongside the sender, the context, and where a login page really lives.
