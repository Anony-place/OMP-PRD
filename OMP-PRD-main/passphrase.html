How passphrases work

Diceware picks words the way a generator picks characters: uniformly, from a known list. Entropy is simply words × log₂(list size). Five words from this list ≈ 51.7 bits; seven ≈ 72.4 bits — memorable and formidable.

When to use one

  • Master passwords for your password manager
  • Device & disk-encryption unlocks
  • Anywhere you must type from memory

Don't hand-pick the words. Humans are terrible random word sources — that's the same trap as "clever" passwords. Generate, then memorize what you got.
