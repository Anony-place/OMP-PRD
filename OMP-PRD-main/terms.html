Last updated: September 29, 2026. By using OopsMyPassword you agree to these terms.

1. Educational purpose

OopsMyPassword ("OMP", "the site") provides educational tools and content about password security. It is not a security audit of your systems, not professional advice, and not a managed security service.

2. Strength scores are estimates, not guarantees

All scores, entropy figures and crack-time estimates produced by OMP are heuristics. They are useful for comparison and learning, and they are not — and cannot be — a guarantee that a password will withstand attack. Whether a password is safe depends on how it was generated, where it is reused, how each service stores it, and the attacker's resources. Use the outputs as guidance, not assurance.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Be careful where you type secrets. While OMP is built to process them locally, you should never enter passwords into websites you do not understand or trust — including this one. Review our privacy policy and, if you wish, verify behavior in your browser's developer tools.
  • You are responsible for the passwords and passphrases you choose to use, where you store them, and how quickly you change them after incidents.
  • Do not use OMP's tools to attack, test, or analyze systems you do not own or lack permission to assess.
