Holehe vs Epieos: Free vs Freemium Email Lookup

Holehe checks 120+ sites free from the terminal; Epieos answers in the browser with Google-linked details. Honest comparison plus when to use each.

Holehe vs Epieos: the short answer

Use Holehe when you want a free, scriptable answer to "where is this email registered?" across 120+ platforms. Use Epieos when you want the answer in a browser, with linked Google account details and phone lookup included (or inspect international phone footprinting via our PhoneInfoga guide). Basic Epieos lookups are free; the deep modules are paid.

Side-by-side comparison

HoleheEpieos
Platforms covered120+Google, Skype, social and app modules
Phone lookupNoYes

ReportsTerminal list, CSV exportWeb report

When Holehe wins

Automation and incident response. Checking one address across many platforms from a script, exporting CSVs for case files, and auditing which services a corporate email is scattered across — all free, all repeatable.

When Epieos wins

Speed for humans. A one-off check on a suspicious address or number, with Google-linked details no CLI tool surfaces as readably. Start free, pay only if the advanced modules earn it.

The practical workflow

Screen with Epieos first when someone hands you an address. If the case matters, confirm and extend with Holehe, then verify the interesting hits by opening each account page yourself. Both live in our OSINT Hub with install steps.
