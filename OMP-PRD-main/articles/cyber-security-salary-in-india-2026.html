If you are planning a cybersecurity career in India, the question is simple: how much can you earn in cyber security in India in 2026? The answer depends on your role, experience, city, certifications, and whether you join a service company or a product company — and the spread is wide: freshers start around ₹3.5–8 LPA (₹25,000–65,000 per month), while senior architects and CISOs cross ₹50 LPA to ₹1 crore+.

The figures below are market ranges compiled from public 2026 Indian salary aggregators (aggregator-reported CTC bands — the same data sources candidates use to negotiate). Actual offers vary by company, city, and negotiation; every number is an estimate, not an official statistic.

Cyber Security Salary in India in 2026

India's cybersecurity hiring has stayed strong through 2026 — industry estimates put the domestic talent shortage at hundreds of thousands of unfilled roles, and certified candidates in metros routinely field multiple offers. That demand is why even fresher bands moved up over the last two years.

Quick Salary Snapshot (₹, 2026)

  • Fresher (0–2 yrs): ₹3.5–8 LPA — ₹25,000–65,000 per month
  • Early career (2–4 yrs): ₹6–15 LPA — ₹55,000–1.25 lakh per month
  • Mid-level (4–7 yrs): ₹12–25 LPA — ₹1–2 lakh per month
  • Senior (7–10 yrs): ₹25–50 LPA — ₹2–4 lakh per month
  • Leadership (CISO/Head of Security): ₹50 LPA – ₹2 Cr+ total compensation

These are CTC bands (cost to company). In-hand salary after PF, professional tax, and deductions is typically 70–85% of CTC at these levels — see "Per month vs LPA" below.

Cyber Security Salary in India by Job Role

1. SOC Analyst (Tier 1 / Tier 2)

The most common fresher entry point: shift-based monitoring, alert triage, incident logging. Certifications like CompTIA Security+ or CEH help clear the bar. Band: ₹3.5–7 LPA at entry, ₹8–14 LPA with 3+ years (L2/L3).

2. Cyber Security Analyst

Vulnerability management, patch tracking, reporting, and control checks — broader than SOC work. Typical range ₹4–12 LPA, with senior analysts and GRC-adjacent roles reaching ₹15 LPA.

3. Penetration Tester / Ethical Hacker

Simulated attacks on applications, networks, and infrastructure to find weaknesses before real attackers do. One of the highest-paid technical tracks: ₹6–20 LPA (aggregators put the Indian average near ₹7 LPA, with CEH juniors starting ₹4.5–8 LPA and OSCP-certified seniors well above ₹25 LPA).

4. Cloud Security Engineer

Cloud configuration review, IAM, workload protection across AWS/Azure/GCP. The fastest-growing band of 2026: ₹8–35 LPA depending on certification depth and cloud scale experience.

5. AppSec / Product Security Engineer

Secure SDLC, code review, and threat modeling inside product companies. Strong application-security candidates command ₹14–35 LPA — this is where product-firm premiums show most clearly.

6. Security Architect / Senior Engineer

Designs the security control set for an organization; OSCP/CISSP preferred at this level. ₹20–55 LPA at 6–10 years.

7. CISO / Head of Security

Owns the security strategy, budget, and regulatory interface. Aggregator averages cluster around ₹40–90 LPA, with top-tier global firms crossing ₹1 crore total compensation at 12+ years.

What Certifications Actually Add

  • CEH: the common fresher differentiator — certified entry candidates report starting packages ₹4.5–8 LPA versus ₹3.5–6 LPA without.
  • OSCP: the biggest single premium in offensive security; aggregator data puts OSCP-certified professionals around ₹27 LPA average, well above the general band.
  • CISSP: expected for architect/manager tracks; mostly a gate rather than a direct raise.
  • Cloud security certs (AWS/Azure Security): unlock the ₹12–35 LPA cloud band faster than any other add-on.

Per Month vs LPA: How to Read an Offer

Indian offers quote CTC per annum (LPA), but candidates search and compare per month. Convert carefully:

  • CTC ÷ 12 is not in-hand. PF (employee + employer share), gratuity accrual, and variable pay sit inside CTC. A ₹6 LPA offer usually lands around ₹35,000–42,000 per month in hand.
  • Variable/bonus can be 5–15% of CTC at mid-level and higher at leadership — ask what is guaranteed.
  • In Indian rupees: all bands on this page are ₹ (INR) CTC, converted to monthly at the gross level.

Frequently asked questions

What is the cyber security salary in India per month?

₹25,000–65,000 per month for freshers (₹3.5–8 LPA CTC), ₹75,000–2 lakh per month at mid-level (4–7 years), and ₹2 lakh+ per month at senior levels. Leadership roles (CISO) reach ₹4–16 lakh per month in total compensation.

What is the cyber security salary for freshers in India?

₹3.5–8 LPA (roughly ₹25,000–65,000 per month) for 0–2 years. SOC Analyst L1 is the most common entry role; CEH or Security+ certification typically moves the offer to the upper half of the band, and product companies pay 20–30% above service-company bands.

What is the government cyber security salary per month?

Entry-level government technical posts sit at Pay Level 7–8 — ₹44,900–50,000 monthly basic, and roughly ₹45,000–1,50,000 per month total after dearness allowance, HRA, and other allowances, depending on grade and posting city.

What is the cyber security salary in India for 2 years experience?

₹6–12 LPA is the common band at 2 years (about ₹55,000–1 lakh per month), depending on whether you stayed in SOC work or moved into penetration testing, cloud, or AppSec. A well-timed switch at this stage is the standard way to jump 40–60%.

What is the cyber security salary in India for 5 years experience?

₹12–25 LPA (₹1–2 lakh per month) for mid-level engineers, testers, and analysts at 5 years. Specializations like cloud security and product security sit at the top of this band; certifications like OSCP or cloud security credentials push beyond it.

How does cyber security salary in India compare to the USA?

Different scales: the US median for information security analysts was $129,180 per year (BLS, May 2025) against an Indian mid-level median near ₹12–25 LPA. Adjusted for cost of living the gap narrows, and remote roles for overseas employers are the common bridge. Full US numbers: Cyber Security Salary in the USA in 2026.
