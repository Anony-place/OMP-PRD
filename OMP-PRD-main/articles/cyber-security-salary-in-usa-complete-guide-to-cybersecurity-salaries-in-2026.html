If you're considering a career in cybersecurity, one of the biggest questions is: how much can you earn in cybersecurity in the USA? The answer depends on your role, experience, location, skills, industry, and certifications.

In 2026, cybersecurity continues to be one of the strongest career fields in technology. According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS), information security analysts had a median annual wage of $129,180 in May 2025, with the highest-paid 10% earning more than $199,850 per year.

Cyber Security Salary in USA in 2026

The average salary you can expect in cybersecurity depends heavily on the position you choose. Entry-level cybersecurity professionals typically earn less than experienced security engineers, penetration testers, security architects, and cybersecurity managers.

For the closest broad occupation tracked by the BLS, information security analysts, the median annual salary was $129,180 in May 2025. The median means half of workers earned more than this amount and half earned less.

Quick Salary Snapshot

  • Median Information Security Analyst Salary: $129,180/year
  • Median Hourly Wage: $62.11/hour
  • Lowest 10%: Less than $75,090/year
  • Highest 10%: More than $199,850/year
  • Projected Employment Growth: 21% from 2025 to 2035

These figures come from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics and provide a useful benchmark for understanding cybersecurity compensation in the United States.

Cybersecurity Salary by Experience Level

Experience is one of the most important factors affecting cybersecurity salaries in the USA. As professionals gain hands-on experience, develop specialized skills, and take on greater responsibility, their earning potential generally increases.

Experience Level Typical Cybersecurity Roles Typical Salary Range
Entry Level SOC Analyst, Junior Security Analyst $60,000 – $90,000
Mid Level Security Analyst, Security Engineer $90,000 – $130,000
Senior Level Senior Security Engineer, Penetration Tester $120,000 – $170,000+
Leadership Security Manager, Security Architect $150,000 – $200,000+
Executive CISO, Chief Security Officer $200,000 – $300,000+

These ranges are general market-oriented estimates rather than official BLS salary categories. Actual compensation can vary significantly by employer, location, specialization, and total compensation package.

Cyber Security Salary in USA by Job Role

Cybersecurity is a broad field. A security analyst, penetration tester, cloud security engineer, and CISO can have very different responsibilities and compensation levels.

1. Cybersecurity Analyst

Cybersecurity analysts monitor systems, investigate security incidents, identify vulnerabilities, and help organizations protect their networks and data.

The BLS classifies information security analysts as professionals who plan and carry out security measures designed to protect an organization's computer networks and systems.

2. Security Engineer

Security engineers typically work on designing, implementing, and maintaining security controls. Their responsibilities can include endpoint security, network security, identity management, cloud security, and security automation.

Because the role often requires stronger technical and engineering skills, experienced security engineers can command salaries above the general cybersecurity analyst benchmark.

3. Penetration Tester

Penetration testers, also called ethical hackers, simulate attacks against systems, applications, networks, and infrastructure to identify security weaknesses before malicious attackers can exploit them.

Strong knowledge of web applications, networking, Linux, cloud platforms, scripting, vulnerability research, and offensive security can increase earning potential in this career path.

4. Cloud Security Engineer

Cloud security has become increasingly important as organizations move applications and infrastructure to cloud platforms. Cloud security professionals work with areas such as identity and access management, cloud configuration, monitoring, application security, and data protection.

5. Cybersecurity Manager

Cybersecurity managers combine technical knowledge with leadership responsibilities. They may manage security teams, security operations, incident response programs, risk management, compliance, and security strategy.

6. Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)

A CISO is a senior executive responsible for an organization's overall cybersecurity strategy. Compensation can be substantially higher than individual contributor roles, particularly at large technology companies, financial institutions, and other organizations with significant security requirements.

Cybersecurity Salary by Location in the USA

Where you work can have a major impact on your cybersecurity salary. Large technology markets and cities with high concentrations of financial, technology, defense, and cloud companies can offer significantly higher compensation.

For example, BLS data for the San Jose metropolitan area reported a mean annual wage of $196,700 for information security analysts in May 2025.

Location Cybersecurity Salary Potential Why It Can Be Higher
California Very High Large technology sector and high concentration of security jobs
New York Very High Finance, technology, consulting, and enterprise security
Washington High Technology companies and government-related organizations
Virginia High Government, defense, and technology employers
Texas High Growing technology and enterprise markets

Location should not be evaluated by salary alone. Cost of living, remote-work availability, taxes, benefits, and career opportunities can significantly change the real value of a compensation package.

What Factors Affect Cyber Security Salary in USA?

There is no single cybersecurity salary in the United States. Employers consider several factors when determining compensation.

Experience

Professional experience is one of the strongest factors. Someone with several years of incident response, cloud security, penetration testing, or security engineering experience can qualify for more advanced positions.

Technical Skills

Skills in areas such as cloud security, application security, penetration testing, identity and access management, detection engineering, threat hunting, and security automation can make candidates more competitive.

Certifications

Certifications can help demonstrate specialized knowledge. Depending on the career path, professionals may consider certifications such as Security+, CySA+, CISSP, OSCP, CCSP, or cloud-security certifications.

Education

A bachelor's degree in cybersecurity, computer science, information technology, or another related field is common for many cybersecurity positions. However, skills, experience, projects, certifications, and practical knowledge can also play an important role.

Industry

Cybersecurity professionals working in financial services, technology, consulting, government, defense, and other security-sensitive industries may encounter different compensation levels.

Is Cybersecurity a High-Paying Career in the USA?

Yes. Cybersecurity can be a high-paying technology career, particularly for professionals who develop specialized technical skills and move into senior or leadership positions.

The BLS reports that information security analysts earned a median annual wage of $129,180 in May 2025. For comparison, the median annual wage across all occupations was $50,980.

The field also has a strong employment outlook. BLS projects employment for information security analysts to grow 21% from 2025 to 2035, much faster than the average for all occupations.

How to Increase Your Cybersecurity Salary

If your goal is to reach a higher cybersecurity salary in the USA, focus on building skills that employers need rather than collecting certifications without practical experience.

  1. Build strong networking fundamentals. Learn TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP, VPNs, firewalls, and network troubleshooting.
  2. Learn Linux and Windows security. Understand operating systems, permissions, authentication, logging, and system hardening.
  3. Develop scripting skills. Python, PowerShell, Bash, and automation skills can make security professionals more productive.
  4. Learn cloud security. Understand AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud security concepts and identity management.
  5. Build practical projects. Create home labs, detection rules, vulnerable applications, security automation, or penetration-testing reports.
  6. Choose a specialization. Areas such as cloud security, application security, offensive security, detection engineering, and incident response can provide strong career paths.
  7. Develop communication skills. Senior cybersecurity roles require professionals to explain technical risks and recommendations to managers and business stakeholders.

Cybersecurity Career Outlook in the USA

The demand for cybersecurity professionals remains strong because organizations increasingly depend on digital infrastructure, cloud services, artificial intelligence, connected systems, and online applications.

According to the BLS, information security analyst employment is projected to increase from approximately 192,900 jobs in 2025 to about 233,400 jobs in 2035, representing 21% growth.

The BLS also projects about 14,100 openings per year for information security analysts on average over the 2025–2035 period.

Frequently Asked Questions About Cyber Security Salary in USA

What is the average cyber security salary in USA?

There is no single official average for every cybersecurity job. For information security analysts, the BLS reported a median annual wage of $129,180 in May 2025.

How much does an entry-level cybersecurity professional make in the USA?

Entry-level salaries vary by location, employer, skills, and job title. Junior security analysts and SOC analysts commonly earn less than experienced security engineers and specialized professionals.

Can cybersecurity professionals make $100,000 a year?

Yes. Experienced cybersecurity professionals can earn $100,000 or more, depending on their role, location, industry, and technical specialization.

Can cybersecurity professionals make $200,000 a year?

Yes. Senior technical professionals, security architects, managers, executives, and highly specialized cybersecurity professionals can reach or exceed $200,000 in total compensation. The BLS reports that the highest 10% of information security analysts earned more than $199,850 in May 2025.

Is cybersecurity worth it in 2026?

Cybersecurity can be a strong career choice in 2026 because the field combines competitive compensation with strong projected employment growth. However, success requires continuous learning because security technologies and threats change rapidly.

What cybersecurity job pays the most?

Senior leadership roles such as CISO can command very high compensation. Among technical roles, experienced security architects, cloud security engineers, application security professionals, and specialized offensive security professionals can also earn high salaries.

Final Thoughts

The cyber security salary in USA can vary widely depending on your experience, job title, location, skills, certifications, and industry. The latest BLS data provides a strong benchmark: information security analysts had a median annual wage of $129,180 in May 2025, while the highest-paid 10% earned more than $199,850.

If you're starting your cybersecurity career, don't focus only on the salary number. Build strong technical fundamentals, gain practical experience, specialize in an in-demand area, and keep improving your skills. Over time, these factors can have a much bigger impact on your earning potential.
